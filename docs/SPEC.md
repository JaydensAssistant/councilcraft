## S53 Settlements System (Jayden's design, 2026-10-13) — REQUIRED AT LAUNCH, ALL SYSTEMS (incl. caravans + raids)
Base: Jayden's "Settlements System" doc (Village Hearth, Guild Ledgers, zoning/sky access/2 glass windows, min volume 5x5x4, physical pathfinding, import (raw + emerald labor fee -> refined) / export (raw -> emeralds), town XP levels 1-10, Guild Charters tech tree, zero zombie-cure buffs, biome specializations, Caravan Posts with simulated travel 1,000+ block routes, random raids as blockades with no defense rewards).
Amendments decided 2026-10-13:
- S53a Raids/blockades: a blockaded village recovers on its own after a set time, at a cost (offline players aren't locked out forever).
- S53b Hearth claim radius: 128 blocks (supersedes 512-640).
- S53c Export caps: very low per-item payout cap, ~half a stack (32) per item per village per day; variety needed to be viable.
- S53d Biome lock-in is intentional: top-tier goods exclusive to their biome, to drive economic wars/truces between factions controlling villages.
- S53e Stuck failsafe: stuck villagers teleport to their own bed/workstation, not the Hearth.
- S53f Villager gear vs loot: Jayden not concerned about keeping exploration meta; no cap required.
- S53g Village control (2026-10-13): voting power = share of village XP each player contributed. Voting power decides control (who the village trades with, etc.). Players can cede or share their voting power with others.

### S66 Demand-driven economy (MAYBE, early draft; Jayden "not in love with it", 2026-10-15) [SUPERSEDED by S67 / S68]
- Create Numismatics + Numismatics: Villager Currency (replaces emeralds in all villager trades with coins, 1 emerald = 1 bevel; Fabric 1.20.1; required on players' computers; closed-source).
- Value comes only from demand. One server-wide stockpile per item, with a cap. Selling to villagers adds to stock while under the cap; buying from villagers draws it down. Prices worsen as stock fills.
- Villager products use up inputs from the shared stock, e.g. a golden carrot takes 1 carrot + 8 gold nuggets, a book takes 3 paper + 1 leather. Recipes are datapack-driven in Settlements.
- A village draws on the shared stock only when it's connected to the rail/caravan network. Optional delivery delay or fee per hop.
- Villager prices = input costs + markup, so villagers set the price ceiling.
- Shopping district incentives: player-set vendor prices, villages stock only vanilla basics, district at the spawn rail hub, optional tax on village trades.
- Open: whether to keep this model at all; whether Settlements owns the stock logic. [SUPERSEDED by S67 / S68]
