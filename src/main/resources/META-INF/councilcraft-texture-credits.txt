Government mold textures

Source: Create: Coins Arise 1.0.2 (Modrinth E3j7uXh3 / yMF1xNuh). Coins Arise
itself is not in the pack (S173 cut it). The textures were taken from that jar
so the molds could be recolored.
Authors: SnackBag.NET Team. Lead developer JX_Snack. Artists Kamilskii and
Kszaczuch.
Licence: GNU LGPL-2.1. The full text is LICENSE-coins-arise-LGPL-2.1.txt in
this directory. These textures are modified copies. No AI image generation
was used.

spur_mold, bevel_mold and sprocket_mold are recolors of
assets/coins_arise/textures/item/spur_casting_mold.png,
bevel_casting_mold.png and sprocket_casting_mold.png. The dark outline
(11, 13, 21) is unchanged. The remaining colours were shifted, darkest to
lightest, onto copper, brass and blue steel.

cog_mold, crown_mold and sun_mold keep the sprocket mold's outline and shading
shape, which makes them a derivative of that LGPL texture. The metal colour
and the mark in the middle (cog, crown, sun) are new pixels drawn by hand.
